What the chart says

1

The IMU is the budget. At 11.00 mA the BNO085 in nine-axis fusion is 75% of today's draw — more than the red LED, the blue LED, the MCU, the radio and everything else combined. Its gyro rail alone is a fixed 7.50 mA in every fusion mode, so dropping the report rate from 100 Hz to our 10 Hz buys almost nothing. Reducing it to the significant-motion detector takes it to 0.54 mA, a 20× cut, and is what makes the 85-day figure possible. Nothing else in the budget has that leverage.

2

The red LED is worth about half a day. 1.50 mA of 14.74 is 10%: 4.5 days becomes 5.0. Removing D6 or R10 is cheap and worth doing on field units, but it is a trim, not a fix. The blue status LED adds a further 0.2 days, and the connected-state cadence we already shipped cut its share from 0.50 mA to 0.03 mA — a bigger proportional win than removing the red one, for no hardware change.

3

The Person Sensor cannot run continuously on a battery. 45 mA takes the node from 4.5 days to 27 hours. Duty-cycled at 250 ms every 10 s it is affordable — but only if its standby current is low, and that number is undocumented. At 1 mA standby the cost is 2.1 mA; at 10 mA it is 10.9 mA and the duty cycle has bought nothing. In the M5 configuration this dominates completely: standby at 1 mA alone drops 85 days to 37. The design conclusion is that it needs a hard power switch — a load switch on its 3.3 V, or the RV-1805 power-switch output — not the standby register.

4

You will not get 2000 mAh. V_BATT reaches VIN through a Schottky, and the AP2112 needs roughly 3.36 V in to hold 3.3 V out. Add the diode drop and the 3.3 V rail starts sagging at about 3.58 V on the cell — perhaps 15–20% of charge still unused. Every figure here uses 1600 mAh. The same diode drop is the suspected cause of the USB-to-battery brownout in §4.2, so one measurement settles two open questions.

5

Two unmeasured inputs can still move the answer. The CH340E is modelled at 0.50 mA on a README note that says only "hundreds of µA to mA"; if it is really 2 mA it is the second-largest load today and the whole M5 floor depends on isolating it. The Apollo3 average without power management is inferred from the poll rate, not measured. Both are one bench session with a current meter.

Model: average current at the 3.3 V rail; an LDO supply, so rail mAh maps roughly 1:1 to cell mAh. Radio assumed advertising throughout, never connected.
